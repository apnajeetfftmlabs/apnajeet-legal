Fair Play Policy

Effective Date: 2 June 2026

ApnaJeet is committed to maintaining a fair, competitive, and enjoyable esports environment for all users.

1. Fair Competition

All users must compete honestly and follow tournament rules. Tournament outcomes must reflect player skill, effort, and performance.

2. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Cheating
  • Hacking
  • Modded Applications
  • Scripts or Automation Tools
  • Exploiting Bugs or Glitches
  • Match Fixing
  • Account Sharing
  • Multiple Account Abuse
  • Fake Tournament Results
  • Fake Screenshots
  • Fraudulent Reward Claims

3. One Device One Account

ApnaJeet enforces a One Device One Account policy to maintain platform integrity.

Users attempting to bypass this restriction may face penalties.

4. Rooted and Modified Devices

Rooted devices, modified operating systems, or tools designed to gain unfair advantages may be restricted from participation.

5. Tournament Verification

ApnaJeet may require tournament verification through:

  • Result Screenshots
  • Game Profile Verification
  • Tournament Review
  • Manual Administrative Checks

6. Community Conduct

Users must behave respectfully within tournament chats and community spaces.

The following are prohibited:

  • Harassment
  • Threats
  • Hate Speech
  • Abusive Language
  • Spam
  • Promotion of Cheats or Hacks
  • Illegal Content

7. Penalties

Violations may result in:

  • Warnings
  • Tournament Disqualification
  • Reward Cancellation
  • Temporary Suspension
  • Permanent Ban

8. Administrative Decisions

All Fair Play investigations and administrative decisions made by ApnaJeet are final.

9. Policy Updates

ApnaJeet reserves the right to update this Fair Play Policy at any time.
