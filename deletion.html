Account Deletion Policy

Effective Date: 2 June 2026

Users have the right to request deletion of their ApnaJeet account.

1. How to Request Deletion

Users may request account deletion by contacting:

apnajeet.fftmlabs@gmail.com

Suggested Subject Line:

Account Deletion Request

2. Information Required

For account verification, users may be asked to provide:

  • Registered Email Address
  • User ID
  • Account Verification Information

3. What Happens After Deletion

Upon successful deletion:

  • Account access will be permanently removed.
  • Profile information may be deleted or anonymized.
  • Tournament participation history may be removed.
  • Coins and Gems will be permanently forfeited.
  • Undelivered rewards may be cancelled.
  • The deleted account may not be recoverable.

4. Data Retention

Certain records may be retained temporarily where required for:

  • Legal Compliance
  • Security Investigations
  • Fraud Prevention
  • Platform Integrity and Abuse Prevention

5. Processing Time

Deletion requests are generally processed within 30 days of successful verification.

6. Future In-App Deletion

ApnaJeet may introduce an in-app account deletion feature in future updates.

7. Contact

Email:
apnajeet.fftmlabs@gmail.com
