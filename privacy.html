Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2 June 2026

Welcome to ApnaJeet.

ApnaJeet is a skill-based esports platform that promotes competitive gaming and rewards players through sponsored prizes, gift cards, vouchers, and redeem codes.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Photo
  • Google Account Information
  • Firebase User ID (UID)
  • Public User ID
  • Game Nickname
  • Favorite Game
  • Tournament Participation Data
  • Device Model
  • Android Version
  • Device Identifier (for security and anti-cheat purposes)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Notification Token
  • Tournament Verification Screenshots

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users through Google Sign-In
  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To organize tournaments and competitions
  • To verify tournament participation and results
  • To prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse
  • To enforce One Device One Account security policies
  • To provide rewards and sponsored prizes
  • To send notifications and updates
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To provide customer support

3. Tournament Verification

Users may submit screenshots for tournament result verification, task verification, reward verification, and anti-fraud checks.

Verification screenshots may be processed through secure third-party services used by ApnaJeet solely for verification and fraud-prevention purposes.

4. Advertising and Analytics

ApnaJeet uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

These services may collect limited information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. Tournament Chat

Users participating in tournaments may communicate through tournament chat features.

Users are responsible for the content they share.

ApnaJeet reserves the right to moderate chat activity and take action against abusive, illegal, offensive, harmful, or inappropriate content.

6. Data Sharing

  • User personal data is never sold.
  • Data is shared only with trusted service providers required for app functionality.
  • Data may be disclosed when required by law.
  • Data may be used to prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats.

ApnaJeet does not sell, rent, or trade user personal information to advertisers or third parties.

7. Security

ApnaJeet implements security measures including:

  • One Device One Account Protection
  • Root Device Detection
  • Anti-Cheat Systems
  • Account Verification Controls

Reasonable security measures are used to protect user information from unauthorized access.

8. No Real Money Transactions

ApnaJeet does not support:

  • Real Money Deposits
  • Cash Withdrawals
  • Betting
  • Wagering
  • Gambling Activities

Coins and Gems are virtual platform rewards and have no real-world monetary value.

9. Age Requirement

ApnaJeet is intended only for users aged 18 years and above.

Users under 18 years of age must not use the platform.

10. Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion by contacting:

apnajeet.fftmlabs@gmail.com

Approved deletion requests will result in the removal or anonymization of applicable user data, subject to legal, security, and fraud-prevention requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

ApnaJeet may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Continued use of the platform after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

Email:
