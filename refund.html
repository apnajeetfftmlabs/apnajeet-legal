Refund Policy

Effective Date: 2 June 2026

ApnaJeet does not accept real-money deposits and does not sell Coins, Gems, or tournament entries for real money.

1. No Real Money Deposits

Users cannot deposit money into ApnaJeet.

Coins and Gems are earned through platform activities such as:

  • Daily Login Streaks
  • Daily Tasks
  • Promotional Activities
  • Platform Rewards

2. Refund Eligibility

Since ApnaJeet does not accept real-money deposits, refund requests are generally not applicable.

3. Tournament Participation

Tournament entries made using virtual Coins or Gems are non-refundable.

Coins and Gems have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash.

4. Reward Distribution

Rewards such as:

  • Google Play Redeem Codes
  • Gift Cards
  • Vouchers
  • Sponsored Rewards

are distributed according to tournament rules and verification requirements.

5. Technical Issues

If a user experiences a technical issue affecting tournament participation or reward delivery, ApnaJeet may review the case individually.

Any compensation or replacement reward remains solely at the discretion of ApnaJeet.

6. Fraud Prevention

Refund-related abuse, false claims, or fraudulent activities may result in:

  • Reward Cancellation
  • Tournament Disqualification
  • Account Suspension
  • Permanent Ban
