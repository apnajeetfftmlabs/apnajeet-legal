Reward Policy

Effective Date: 2 June 2026

This Reward Policy explains how rewards are distributed on ApnaJeet.

1. Reward Types

ApnaJeet may provide rewards including:

  • Google Play Redeem Codes
  • Gift Cards
  • Vouchers
  • Sponsored Promotional Rewards
  • Gaming Rewards

2. Reward Eligibility

Rewards are available only to eligible tournament winners who comply with platform rules and Fair Play requirements.

Eligibility may depend on:

  • Tournament ranking
  • Result verification
  • Compliance with platform policies
  • Successful anti-cheat review

3. Verification Process

Reward claims may require:

  • Tournament result verification
  • Screenshot verification
  • Account review
  • Fair Play compliance checks

Failure to provide requested verification may result in reward cancellation.

4. No Cash Rewards

ApnaJeet does not provide:

  • Cash Withdrawals
  • Bank Transfers
  • UPI Payouts
  • Conversion of Coins or Gems into Real Money

Coins and Gems are virtual platform rewards and have no monetary value.

5. Reward Availability

Rewards are subject to:

  • Availability
  • Sponsorship Support
  • Tournament Rules
  • Platform Policies

ApnaJeet reserves the right to replace a reward with an equivalent alternative if necessary.

6. Delivery Time

Reward delivery times may vary depending on tournament verification and administrative review.

ApnaJeet will make reasonable efforts to deliver rewards promptly after successful verification.

7. Fraud Prevention

Fraudulent reward claims may result in:

  • Reward Cancellation
  • Tournament Disqualification
  • Account Suspension
  • Permanent Ban

Examples of prohibited behavior include:

  • Fake screenshots
  • Manipulated tournament results
  • Multiple account abuse
  • Cheating or hacking

8. Final Decision

All reward-related decisions made by ApnaJeet administrators are final.

9. Policy Updates

ApnaJeet may update this Reward Policy at any time.

Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of future updates.
